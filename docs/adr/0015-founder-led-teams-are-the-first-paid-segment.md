# Founder-Led Teams of Two to Eight Are the First Paid Segment

The first team product targets software teams of two to eight people led directly by a founder or technical lead. These teams already have several members using one or more AI coding tools in parallel and often advance at least three development tasks that run for hours or days. Heavy individual developers enter through the free local product and may upgrade when they form a team. Small groups inside enterprises are a later market so the first phase does not simultaneously carry deployment, compliance, identity, and procurement requirements.

## 中文

# 首个付费客户是 2–8 人的创始人直管团队

首版团队产品面向由创始人或技术负责人直接带队的 2–8 人软件团队。这些团队已经让多个成员并行使用一个或多个 AI 编码工具，并经常同时推进至少三项持续数小时或数天的开发工作。重度个人开发者通过免费本地版进入并可能在组队后升级；企业内部小团队属于后续市场，避免第一阶段同时承担部署、合规、身份和采购要求。
