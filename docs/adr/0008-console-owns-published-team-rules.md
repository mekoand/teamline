# The Console Owns the Authoritative Record of Published Team Rules

The team console is the sole authoritative source for team operating rules published in it and for their version history. Repositories, coding tools, and other systems may continue to store and maintain rules within their own scopes. The console neither co-edits the same rule with those sources nor automatically copies discovered external rules into editable team rules. External rules participate in a goal through an effective rule set that preserves their source and version.

## 中文

# 控制台拥有已发布团队规则的权威记录

团队控制台是在其中发布的团队运行规则及其版本历史的唯一权威来源。仓库、编码工具和其他系统可以继续保存并维护各自范围内的规则，控制台不与这些来源共同编辑同一条规则，也不把发现到的外部规则自动复制成可编辑的团队规则。外部规则通过保留来源与版本的生效规则集参与具体工作目标。
