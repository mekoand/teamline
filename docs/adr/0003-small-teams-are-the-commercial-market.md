# Small AI Coding Teams Are the Primary Commercial Market

The main commercial customers are small AI coding teams formed by solo founders, early-stage startups, and small groups inside larger companies. They need to coordinate multiple AI coding workflows and have clear delivery, budget, and responsibility requirements. Heavy individual developers remain important design partners and the adoption entry point, but they do not represent the full market. This choice prevents the long-term product from shrinking into a recovery tool for a small number of power users.

## 中文

# 小型 AI 编码团队是主要商业市场

产品以个人创业者、早期创业团队和企业内部小组构成的小型 AI 编码团队为主要商业客户，因为它们需要同时协调多个 AI 编码工作流，并有明确的交付、预算和责任需求。重度个人开发者仍是重要的设计伙伴和采用入口，但不再代表产品的完整市场；这一选择避免把长期产品压缩成只服务少数 Power User 的恢复工具。
