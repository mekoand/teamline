# The Local Personal Product Is Free and the Team Product Is Paid

Individuals can create and advance their own work goals locally for free while keeping code, model sessions, and credentials on their devices. Commercial revenue comes from the shared team control plane, published team rules, resource coordination, authorization and exceptions, handoffs, and audit capabilities. This boundary lowers adoption friction and builds individual trust while charging teams for the additional value created by multi-person governance instead of artificially weakening the personal product.

The first commercial test uses a subscription of USD 99 per team workspace per month, including five active members, with additional members provisionally priced at USD 20 per person per month. The product does not charge per goal or take a share of model spend. This price is a hypothesis to validate through paid pilots, not a confirmed final price.

## 中文

# 本地个人端免费，团队产品付费

个人用户可以免费在本机创建和推进自己的工作目标，并保留代码、模型会话与凭据在本地；商业收入来自团队共享控制面、已发布团队规则、资源协调、授权与例外、交接和审计能力。这个边界既降低个人采用门槛并建立信任，也让团队为多人治理产生的额外价值付费，而不是人为削弱个人端。

首轮商业验证采用每个团队工作区每月 99 美元的订阅，包含 5 名活跃成员，超出后暂按每人每月 20 美元计费。产品不按目标数量收费，也不从模型消耗中抽成。该价格是需要通过付费试点验证的假设，而不是已经确认的最终价格。
