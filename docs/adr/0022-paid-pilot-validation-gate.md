# Validate the Product Hypothesis with Five Paid Team Pilots

Before the team pilot, freeze the complexity tiers without making complexity a product entry requirement for managed execution. Any work with a clear boundary, code changes, and an acceptance method can become a goal. An estimate of at least two hours or two execution stages is used only to separate long-running and short work in advance for analysis. Five teams first record a comparable two-week baseline, then pay USD 99 and use the product for four weeks. The hypothesis passes only if at least three teams place more than half of eligible long-running work into managed execution; reliable review-ready goals per owner per week rise by at least 30% within the frozen complexity tiers; first-pass acceptance within three business days does not fall below baseline while major rework, unplanned coordination time, and rule exceptions do not materially worsen after normalization by goal; and at least three teams pay another USD 99 after the pilot. Teams may not alter the tiers after the pilot or inflate results by arbitrarily splitting goals.

## 中文

# 用五支团队的付费试点验证产品假设

团队试点开始前冻结复杂度分层，但不把复杂度作为进入受控执行的产品门槛。所有具有明确边界、会产生代码变更并有验收方式的工作都可以形成目标；预计至少两小时或两个执行阶段只用于预先区分长程工作与短工作，并分别观察效果。五支团队先记录两周同口径基线，再实际支付 99 美元使用四周。产品假设成立需要同时满足：至少三支团队把一半以上符合目标场景的长程工作放进受控执行；按复杂度分层后每名负责人每周可靠进入待验收的目标数提高至少 30%；三个工作日内的一次验收通过率不低于基线，重大返工率、计划外协调时间和规则例外率按目标归一化后不明显恶化；至少三支团队在试点结束后再次实际支付 99 美元续费。试点结束后不得调整分层或通过任意拆小目标提高结果。
